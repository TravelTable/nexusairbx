# NexusRBX Design Anti-Guide

This document is a negative design authority. It overrides `vercel-saas-design-research-expanded.md` and any instruction that treats that research as a design brief, component guide, token library, mood board, reference library, or source of inspiration.

## Core rule

Do not reproduce, imitate, adapt, remix, average, borrow from, or use as inspiration anything contained in `vercel-saas-design-research-expanded.md`.

Treat that entire document as a record of what the new NexusRBX system must avoid. Changing a few colours, swapping a font, or slightly altering spacing does not make a traced structure independent.

## Excluded source material

Do not derive any of the following from the negative research:

- visual archetypes, art direction, or overall character;
- colour palettes, gradients, tints, accents, or theme recipes;
- font families, pairings, scales, weights, tracking, or line heights;
- spacing scales, gutters, margins, section rhythm, or density targets;
- containers, grids, sidebars, hero proportions, or page sequences;
- cards, pills, badges, tabs, tables, navigation bars, or application shells;
- radii, borders, shadows, blur, glass, or layer treatments;
- buttons, inputs, dropdowns, filters, icons, or control dimensions;
- component, interaction, loading, hover, or motion treatments;
- responsive collapse patterns or reconstructed approximate values;
- site-specific preserve/change recommendations or hybrids assembled from examples.

Accessibility and familiar platform behaviour remain requirements, but the research must not prescribe their visual execution.

## Generic SaaS exclusions

Do not fall back to:

- Vercel-style black-and-white minimalism;
- interchangeable early-stage SaaS landing pages;
- oversized centred hero copy followed by a dashboard screenshot;
- uniform rounded-card grids;
- excessive pills, badges, and low-contrast borders;
- neutral sans-serif typography without a Nexus-specific reason;
- blue or purple chosen merely as a familiar technology accent;
- decorative gradients, blobs, glow, glass, or auroras;
- copied developer-tool dashboards, terminal atmosphere, or command palettes;
- repeated feature-card sections with identical visual weight;
- a synthesis of Linear, Vercel, Stripe, Notion, or similar products.

## Required replacement approach

Design from the actual product, audience, brand position, runtime truth, and workflow. The finished system must have an independent:

1. visual concept;
2. colour system;
3. typography system;
4. spacing system;
5. workflow-shaped layout system;
6. component language;
7. meaning-led motion language;
8. responsive task model.

Usability conventions may remain where needed for comprehension, accessibility, or platform consistency. Their visual execution must still be independently designed.

## Rejection checklist

- No palette or colour relationship came from the negative research.
- No font choice or type scale came from it.
- No spacing, radius, shadow, grid, or container value was copied from it.
- No page structure or component composition reconstructs one of its examples.
- No design was created by combining several examples.
- The result does not resemble a generic Vercel-era SaaS template.
- Every major visual decision is justified by NexusRBX users, Roblox project work, or Studio review.
- The result remains recognisable when compared beside the researched websites.

If any check fails, revise the design rather than rationalising the similarity.

## Final instruction

Build a completely separate visual system. Use `vercel-saas-design-research-expanded.md` only to understand what must not be done.
